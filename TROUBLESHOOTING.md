# Vigil troubleshooting

- Missing sensors are unavailable, not evidence of good health. Platform-specific temperature and power readings may not exist.
- External snapshots are optional. Malformed checks are marked as invalid; fresh valid warnings are retained.
- Use density, placement and panelWidth to fit your screen. Prefer region placement when a fixed bar would overlap another module.

Use the README for settings, dependencies, privacy and sources. For a bug report, include your device, MagicMirror and Node versions, the observed behavior and a minimal configuration with secrets removed. Do not post private feeds, tokens, precise home coordinates or network snapshots.
